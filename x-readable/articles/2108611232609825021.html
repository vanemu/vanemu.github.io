“SEO is a long game.”

「SEO 是一场长跑。」

That’s what you’re told when your competitors have years of content, rankings for thousands of keywords, and a decade’s worth of backlinks—a gap you can’t seem to close.

竞争对手有多年内容、成千上万关键词排名、十年外链——差距好像永远补不上时，别人就会这么跟你说。

So you find a keyword, write another article, and wait.

于是你找一个词，再写一篇文章，然后等。

Then repeat the process, one page at a time.

接着重复，一次一页。

But it doesn’t have to work that way anymore.

但现在不必再这样了。

With programmatic SEO, you build a system that turns your data into pages for hundreds of related searches, without creating each one by hand.

用程序化 SEO，你搭建一套系统，把数据变成覆盖成百上千相关搜索的页面，而不必手工逐页写。

One layout. Different data. A page for each specific need.

一套版式。不同数据。每个具体需求一页。

Zapier shows what this looks like at scale. A 2026 industry article reports 800,000 pages, rankings for 1.3 million keywords, and 16.2 million monthly organic visits.

Zapier 展示了规模化长什么样。一篇 2026 年行业文章称：80 万页、130 万关键词排名、月自然访问 1620 万。

Here’s how to build a system for your own business.

下面是如何为自己的业务搭这样一套系统。

1. What Is Programmatic SEO?

1. 什么是程序化 SEO？

Programmatic SEO is a way to automatically create many useful pages, each targeting a specific search.

程序化 SEO 是一种自动创建大量有用页面的方式，每一页瞄准一次具体搜索。

Travel sites use it for hotels in different cities. Property platforms use it for homes in different neighborhoods. Online stores use it for product categories and combinations of attributes.

旅游站用它做各城市酒店；房产平台用它做各社区房源；网店用它做品类与属性组合。

A hotel page for Austin lists hotels in Austin. A page for Boston lists a different set of hotels. Both can use the same layout, with different information pulled into it.

奥斯汀酒店页列出奥斯汀酒店；波士顿页列出另一批。两页可用同一版式，只是填入不同信息。

You create the layout once, then use your data to generate the variations.

版式只做一次，再用数据生成变体。

This makes lower-volume, more specific search queries worth targeting: each page takes less work to produce, and traffic from these searches can add up across the site.

这让低量、更具体的搜索也值得做：每页生产成本更低，这些流量加总起来也能可观。

2. Examples of Companies Using Programmatic SEO

2. 使用程序化 SEO 的公司案例

1/Zapier

1/Zapier

Zapier creates pages for individual apps and app pairs, such as Google Sheets and Slack.

Zapier 为单个 App 以及 App 组合做页面，例如 Google Sheets 与 Slack。

The pattern is simple: “Connect [App A] with [App B].” Each pair page shows workflows, triggers, and actions for those particular tools.

模式很简单：「Connect [App A] with [App B]。」每个组合页展示这些工具的工作流、触发器与动作。

Someone searching for a way to connect their tools can discover Zapier through a page built around that exact task.

想找办法连接工具的人，会通过围绕这一确切任务建的页面发现 Zapier。

2/Canva

2/Canva

Canva uses patterns such as “[style] [design type] templates.”

Canva 使用类似「[style] [design type] templates」的模式。

Its professional resume templates page gives visitors designs they can browse and customize. Other pages offer templates for different design types, styles, and occasions, all with a consistent browsing experience.

专业简历模板页让访客浏览并自定义设计；其他页覆盖不同设计类型、风格与场合，浏览体验一致。

The search leads naturally into the product: find a design, open it, and start editing.

搜索自然导向产品：找到设计、打开、开始编辑。

3/Wise

3/Wise

Wise uses the pattern “[currency A] to [currency B].”

Wise 使用「[currency A] to [currency B]」模式。

Its USD to EUR page includes a converter, exchange-rate history, and common conversion amounts. Each currency pair brings different data into the same layout.

USD to EUR 页包含换算器、汇率历史与常见金额。每个货币对把不同数据填进同一版式。

Visitors can complete the conversion immediately. For someone planning an international transfer, sending money with Wise may be the next step.

访客可以立刻完成换算。计划国际转账的人，下一步很可能就是用 Wise 打款。

All three companies build pages around recurring customer needs. For your business, the question is: What are your prospective customers searching for, and how can you help?

三家公司都围绕反复出现的客户需求建页。对你的业务，问题是：潜在客户在搜什么，你怎么帮到他们？

3. How to Get Started with Programmatic SEO

3. 如何开始程序化 SEO

Start with one type of page you can make genuinely useful. You need a recurring search pattern, different material for each variation, and a clear connection to what your business offers.

从一种你能真正做好的页面类型起步。你需要：反复出现的搜索模式、每个变体不同的素材，以及与业务供给的清晰连接。

For this walkthrough, imagine an email marketing business building a library of email templates. People looking for help with onboarding, follow-ups, and webinar invitations may also need a tool to send and manage those emails.

本教程假设一家邮件营销公司在建邮件模板库。找 onboarding、跟进、网络研讨会邀请帮助的人，往往也需要发送与管理这些邮件的工具。

a. Research Keywords

a. 研究关键词

Look for searches with a part that stays the same and a part that changes:

找「一部分固定、一部分会变」的搜索：

・ Sales follow-up email template

Sales follow-up email template

・ Customer onboarding email template

Customer onboarding email template

・ Webinar invitation email template

Webinar invitation email template

“Email template” stays the same. The situation changes.

「Email template」不变，场景在变。

Check the search results to understand what people expect. In this case, they need emails they can copy and adapt, so a page explaining email marketing in general would miss the point.

看搜索结果理解期望。这里他们要的是可复制改编的邮件，所以泛谈邮件营销的页面会答非所问。

Choose opportunities by asking:

筛选机会时问：

・ Would someone making this search be a potential customer?

搜这个词的人会是潜在客户吗？

・ Can we give them a useful answer or asset?

我们能给出有用答案或资产吗？

・ Do we have enough distinct material to support the different pages?

我们有没有足够差异化的素材支撑不同页面？

Low-volume keywords can still be worth targeting when they attract potential customers and the pages are economical to create. Combine similar searches when one page can answer them well.

低量词在吸引潜在客户、且页面生产成本低时仍值得做。一个页面能答好多个相近搜索时，就合并。

NoimosAI can help research keyword opportunities and the pages already ranking for those searches.

NoimosAI 可协助研究关键词机会以及已在排名的页面。

Example prompt for NoimosAI:

给 NoimosAI 的示例 prompt：

Find 20–30 long-tail keyword candidates following the pattern “[use case] email template” across a broad range of marketing and customer communication scenarios relevant to our business. For each keyword, check US search volume, keyword difficulty, and search intent. Group keywords that should share one page, and prioritize the most useful page opportunities. Mark unavailable metrics as unknown rather than estimating them.

围绕模式「[use case] email template」，在与我们业务相关的广泛营销与客户沟通场景中，找出 20–30 个长尾候选词。对每个词查美国搜索量、难度与意图。把应共享一页的词分组，并按最有用机会排序。拿不到的指标标为 unknown，不要估算。

Review the suggestions and choose one pattern for the first batch.

审阅建议，为第一批选一个模式。

b. Collect Data

b. 收集数据

Gather what will make each page worth visiting.

收集能让每页值得访问的材料。

For an email library, that means original sample emails, subject lines, and practical guidance for each situation. A sales follow-up might recap a demo and propose a next step. An onboarding email might help a new customer set up an account.

对邮件库来说，就是各场景的原创样例邮件、主题行与实用指引。销售跟进可能复盘 demo 并提议下一步；onboarding 可能帮新客户完成开户。

Changing the title alone would not serve those different needs. The examples themselves need to change.

只改标题满足不了这些不同需求——样例本身必须变。

Start with your own resources: existing templates, product information, customer questions, or work you have permission to share. Depending on the page type, you might also use official records, licensed APIs, permitted public data, or manual research.

从自有资源起步：现有模板、产品信息、客户问题，或有权分享的作品。按页面类型，也可能用官方记录、授权 API、允许的公开数据或人工调研。

Keep sources and review dates alongside information that can change, such as prices, product features, or local listings.

对价格、功能、本地列表等会变的信息，一并保留来源与审阅日期。

c. Organize the Database

c. 整理数据库

A spreadsheet is enough to start. Use one row for each page and columns for the material it needs.

从表格起步就够。一页一行，列是该页需要的材料。

This row becomes a sales follow-up page. A separate onboarding row becomes an onboarding page, using its own sample emails and instructions.

这一行变成销售跟进页；另一行 onboarding 用自己的样例与说明。

Give each row a stable ID so your import tool can match it to the existing page. Check for duplicate URLs, missing material, and searches that overlap before generating anything.

给每行稳定 ID，方便导入工具匹配已有页。生成前检查重复 URL、缺失素材与重叠搜索。

d. Generate Pages from a Template

d. 从模板生成页面

Build one page layout first. For the email library, it could contain:

先做一套版式。邮件库可以包含：

・ When to use these emails

何时使用这些邮件

・ Subject lines and copyable email examples

主题行与可复制样例

・ What to customize before sending

发送前要改什么

・ Related email templates

相关邮件模板

The layout can stay consistent across the library. The examples and guidance come from each page’s row.

版式可在整库保持一致；样例与指引来自每页那一行。

Use NoimosAI to help turn the prepared material into page copy.

用 NoimosAI 把准备好的材料转成页面文案。

Example prompt for NoimosAI:

给 NoimosAI 的示例 prompt：

Using this template and data, create an SEO-optimized page targeting the keyword “sales follow-up email template”. Analyze the top-ranking pages for this keyword and address the search intent.

用这份模板和数据，创建瞄准关键词「sales follow-up email template」的 SEO 优化页。分析该词排名靠前的页面，并回应搜索意图。

Review the first page before generating more. Can someone quickly find an appropriate email, copy it, and understand what to change?

生成更多之前先审第一页：人能否很快找到合适邮件、复制，并明白要改什么？

Then connect the spreadsheet to your publishing setup. For WordPress, one option is to export a CSV and use WP All Import:

再把表格接到发布系统。WordPress 可选导出 CSV 并用 WP All Import：

・ Choose the page or post type you want to create.

选择要创建的页面或文章类型。

・ Map the columns to the title, body, URL slug, and any custom fields used by your layout.

把列映射到标题、正文、URL slug 以及版式用到的自定义字段。

・ Use the stable page ID as the import’s unique identifier.

用稳定的页面 ID 作为导入唯一标识。

・ Preview a small batch and import it as drafts for review.

预览小批量，以草稿导入供审阅。

The writing step prepares the content; the CMS and import tool create the pages. Set up the layout and field mappings for your site, and check how repeat imports update existing content.

写作步骤准备内容；CMS 与导入工具创建页面。为站点配置版式与字段映射，并检查重复导入如何更新已有内容。

e. Maintain Quality as You Scale

e. 规模化时保持质量

Publish a small pilot, such as five to ten pages, and check three things:

先发小试点（如 5–10 页），检查三件事：

Does each page deliver what the searcher needs?

每页是否交付搜索者需要的东西？

An email page needs usable examples. A comparison needs meaningful differences. A converter needs a working calculation. Skip combinations where you have little to offer beyond a different heading.

邮件页需要可用样例；对比页需要有意义的差异；换算器需要能算。素材几乎只剩换标题的组合就跳过。

Can people and search engines use the pages?

人和搜索引擎能不能用这些页？

Check the mobile experience, links, indexability, canonical URLs, and sitemap entries. Link related pages so visitors can continue to the next useful resource. Publication alone does not guarantee indexing.

检查移动体验、链接、可索引性、canonical、sitemap。把相关页互链，方便继续浏览。上线本身不保证被收录。

Do the pages attract potential customers?

这些页有没有吸引潜在客户？

Track impressions and clicks, then signups, inquiries, or other actions that matter to your business. Give visitors a relevant next step, such as using your tool to send the email they just prepared.

跟踪展示与点击，再跟踪注册、询盘等业务动作。给访客相关下一步，例如用你的工具发送刚准备的邮件。

Expand the patterns that show promise. If visitors arrive but struggle to use the page, improve it before multiplying the same problem.

扩展有前景的模式。若有人来了却用不了页面，先改好，再别把同一问题放大。

Google’s spam policies target large-scale content created mainly to manipulate rankings with little value for users. Automating production does not remove the need for distinct, useful content. Keep the underlying information current as the library grows.

Google 垃圾政策针对主要为操纵排名、对用户价值很少的大规模内容。自动化生产并不免除「内容要有差异、有用」的要求。库长大时，底层信息也要保持更新。

4. Programmatic SEO Templates

4. 程序化 SEO 模板

Choose a pattern that matches both what customers search for and the information or assets you can provide.

选一个既匹配客户搜索、又匹配你能提供的信息/资产的模式。

Templates

模板类

・ Pattern: “[use case] template”

模式：「[use case] template」

・ Search example: “customer onboarding email template”

搜索例：「customer onboarding email template」

・ Why it works: The searcher wants a starting point they can use immediately. A consistent page layout can serve many use cases, while each page contains different examples, subject lines, and customization guidance. Your collection of original templates supplies the variations.

为何管用：搜索者要立刻能用的起点。统一版式可服务多种场景，而每页有不同样例、主题行与定制指引。你的原创模板集提供变体。

Comparisons

对比类

・ Pattern: “[product A] vs [product B]”

模式：「[product A] vs [product B]」

・ Search example: “Asana vs Trello”

搜索例：「Asana vs Trello」

・ Why it works: The searcher is choosing between options. A shared set of criteria makes many product pairs easier to compare, while verified features, prices, limitations, and use cases change with each pair. Each page should explain the differences that affect the decision.

为何管用：搜索者在选项间抉择。共享标准让许多产品对更容易比；功能、价格、限制与用例随对变化。每页应讲清影响决策的差异。

Integrations

集成类

・ Pattern: “[app A] + [app B] integration”

模式：「[app A] + [app B] integration」

・ Search example: “Google Sheets Slack integration”

搜索例：「Google Sheets Slack integration」

・ Why it works: The searcher wants two tools to work together. A catalog of supported integrations can generate pages with the same structure but different workflows, triggers, setup instructions, and limitations. Each page shows how an integration you support helps users complete a specific task.

为何管用：搜索者想让两工具协同。支持的集成目录可用同一结构生成页，但工作流、触发、设置与限制不同。每页展示你支持的集成如何完成具体任务。

Locations

地点类

・ Pattern: “[service] in [location]”

模式：「[service] in [location]」

・ Search example: “coworking spaces in Austin”

搜索例：「coworking spaces in Austin」

・ Why it works: The searcher needs options available in a particular place. Location-based records let one layout serve different areas, with different listings, addresses, amenities, and availability. Accurate local information gives each page a reason to exist beyond changing the city name.

为何管用：搜索者要特定地点的选项。基于地点的记录让一套版式服务不同区域，列表、地址、设施与可用性不同。准确本地信息让每页有存在理由，而不只是换城市名。

Personas

人群类

・ Pattern: “[solution] for [audience]”

模式：「[solution] for [audience]」

・ Search example: “project management software for architects”

搜索例：「project management software for architects」

・ Why it works: The searcher wants a solution that fits their profession or situation. A shared layout can organize benefits and examples across audiences, while the workflows, requirements, and supporting evidence change. Create a page only when you have something specific to say about that audience’s needs.

为何管用：搜索者要贴合职业/情境的方案。共享版式可组织利益与案例，而工作流、要求与证据随人群变。只在对该人群有具体话可说时建页。

Examples

案例类

・ Pattern: “[content type] examples for [use case]”

模式：「[content type] examples for [use case]」

・ Search example: “welcome email examples for SaaS”

搜索例：「welcome email examples for SaaS」

・ Why it works: The searcher wants inspiration before creating something. A tagged collection of examples can supply different selections for different uses, all presented in the same format. Add a short explanation of what makes each example useful, and distinguish real examples from illustrative ones.

为何管用：搜索者创作前要灵感。带标签的案例集可用同一格式为不同用途提供不同精选。加一句说明每个案例为何有用，并区分真实案例与示意。

Conversions

换算类

・ Pattern: “[unit A] to [unit B]”

模式：「[unit A] to [unit B]」

・ Search example: “centimeters to inches”

搜索例：「centimeters to inches」

・ Why it works: The searcher wants an immediate answer. A common calculator layout can serve many unit pairs, with the formula, labels, and example values changing for each one. Accurate calculations and a usable tool make the page valuable even when little written explanation is needed.

为何管用：搜索者要立刻答案。通用计算器版式可服务许多单位对，公式、标签与示例值随对变化。准确计算与好用工具让页面有价值，即便文字不多。

Directories

目录类

・ Pattern: “[category] with [attribute]”

模式：「[category] with [attribute]」

・ Search example: “CRM tools with offline access”

搜索例：「CRM tools with offline access」

・ Why it works: The searcher wants a shortlist that meets a requirement. A database of verified listings and attributes can supply different selections using the same comparison layout. Publish combinations with enough relevant options and detail to help someone choose.

为何管用：搜索者要满足某条件的短名单。经验证的列表与属性库可用同一对比版式给出不同精选。发布有足够相关选项与细节、能帮人做选择的组合。

Zapier shows the scale this approach can reach: a 2026 industry article reports 800,000 pages and 16.2 million monthly organic visits. Its programmatic integration pages illustrate the strategy: build a system that scales beyond writing every page by hand.

Zapier 展示了这套打法能到的规模：2026 年行业文章称 80 万页、月自然访问 1620 万。其程序化集成页说明策略：建一套能规模化的系统，而不是手工写每一页。

Start with one search pattern your customers already use. Build a few pages that answer it well, then expand when the results justify it. Over time, those pages can become a growing set of ways to discover your business, right when someone needs what you offer.

从客户已在用的一个搜索模式起步。先做好几页，结果证明值得再扩展。时间久了，这些页会成为在有人需要你时发现你的一整套入口。
