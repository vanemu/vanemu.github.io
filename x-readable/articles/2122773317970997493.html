After a few weeks with the iPhone Duo, I love it, but the design being so different from typical iPhones presents a few more speed bumps in day to day life than I expected, so I’ve been taking a crack at fixing them with 3D printing and thought I’d share what I’ve been running into and some free files to fix ’em.

用了几周 iPhone Duo 之后，我很喜欢它，但设计和普通 iPhone 差别太大，日常里碰到的磕绊比我想的多。于是我开始用 3D 打印来解决，想把遇到的问题和一些免费的打印文件分享出来。

绘图垫

I vibe-coded a little drawing app for my Duo since I couldn’t find an app I really loved (drawing apps are truly the modern to do list app in terms of people building their own, maybe I’ll release it one day) and I ended up being really surprised how much I loved using the Duo with the Apple Pencil. The nano-texture coating on the inner Duo display is actually so nice to write on.

因为找不到特别喜欢的绘图 App，我给 Duo 随手做了一个小绘图应用（现在人人都爱自己做绘图 App，简直成了新时代的待办清单；也许有一天我会发布它）。没想到用 Duo 搭配 Apple Pencil 会这么舒服。内屏的纳米纹理涂层写起来真的很好。

… except the camera bump makes the thing rock like crazy when you place it on a table to draw. So my friend and viral mascot Charlie Chapman and I dueled on designing 3D printable pads so the Duo could sit on something that would make it flat. His is sleek, compact, and allows charging, while I wanted something that was a bit wider/taller so you could have a place to rest your hand when writing at the extremities.

……只是摄像头凸起让它放在桌上画画时会晃得很厉害。于是我和朋友、也是那个出了名的吉祥物 Charlie Chapman 较上了劲，各自设计 3D 打印垫，让 Duo 能平放。他的方案更纤薄紧凑，还能充电；我想要更宽、更高一点，好在写到屏幕边缘时有地方搁手。

Some features:

一些特点：

  • Finger holes to easily get the Duo back out
    指孔，方便把 Duo 取出来
  • Padding around the sides so if you’re drawing at the extremities of the Duo your hand has somewhere to rest (and only pads the sides you’ll actually use, with left and right handed versions available)
    两侧有垫高，画到边缘时手有地方搁（只垫实际会用到的那一侧，提供左手和右手版本）
  • Wedge design (like an old MacBook Air) so even if you slide your hand off the bottom a bit it moves nicely onto your desk
    楔形设计（像老款 MacBook Air），手稍微滑出底部也能顺势落到桌面上
  • Apple Pencil slot (available even when the Duo is in it which I use more than I expected)
    Apple Pencil 槽（Duo 放进去时也能用，使用频率比我预想的高）
  • Roomy power button cutout, as well as speaker/mic cutouts
    电源键开孔比较宽裕，也留了扬声器和麦克风开孔

It’s made sketching on the Duo so much more comfortable.

有了它，在 Duo 上素描舒服多了。

Link to download. Print at the lowest layer height you can so the wedge shape doesn’t have stair-stepping (can the mainstream printers support non-planar printing yet c’mon). I just printed it at 0.04 mm (had to ignore a warning in my slicer) and let it do the 7 hours overnight, but even at 0.12 it’s not bad at all.

下载链接。尽量用最低层高打印，避免楔面出现明显阶梯（主流打印机支持非平面打印了吗，真是时候了）。我用 0.04 mm 打的（切片软件报了警告我没理），隔夜打了 7 个小时，不过 0.12 mm 其实也不差。

桌面 / 开发支架

A lot of the time the Duo sits on my desk as an iOS dev device, and I haven’t found a nice stand for it yet, despite accumulating quite a few over the years. The Duo is just such a unique shape that maybe you can find a stand that works well in one pose, but in another it’s unstable and weird. As a developer you need one that works in all of them.

很多时候 Duo 就摆在我桌上当 iOS 开发机用，这些年攒了不少支架，却还没找到真正合适的。Duo 的外形太特别，可能某个姿态勉强能用，换个姿态就又晃又别扭。作为开发者，需要一个在各种姿态下都能用的支架。

The bad:

现有支架的问题：

  • Traditional phone stands (that just have a little shelf) aren’t very wide so they doesn’t cover the full width of the phone when closed, and when open they feel worryingly wobbly if you tap the edges of the Duo
    传统手机支架（只有一条小托沿）不够宽，合上时撑不住整机宽度；打开后再点边缘，晃得让人担心
  • They have a lip with no cutouts at the bottom so you can’t plug it in over USB-C (which is really handy to be able to do for development)
    底部挡边没有开孔，插不了 USB-C（开发时这很常用）
  • If you open the phone and put it in portrait, most stands are short and don’t support the height of the device very well, so tapping near the top of the Duo in portrait causes the whole thing to either flip over or have the Duo slide off
    打开后竖放时，多数支架太矮，撑不住机身高度，一点靠近顶部，整架就会翻倒，或者 Duo 滑下来
  • The stands that do handle portrait well have an extra “hook” around the shelf at the bottom, that kinda locks the phone from being able to tip, but that makes it so you can’t open and close the Duo easily because that extra lip gets in the way!
    竖放表现好的支架，托沿上往往还有一道“挂钩”防止倾倒，可这道沿又会挡住，让 Duo 很难开合
  • They’re not made with the Duo’s ports in mind, so depending on whether the Duo is opened or closed, it’s hard to plug in or the speaker/mic is blocked
    没有按 Duo 的接口来设计，开合不同状态下，要么不好插线，要么挡住扬声器或麦克风
  • Floating MagSafe stands don’t really work at all when the device is open, as the magnetic connection is just on one side so the device slowly rotates like a clock
    悬浮式 MagSafe 支架在打开时基本不可用：磁力只吸住一侧，设备会像时钟一样慢慢转下去

So with my swing at it, I made these changes:

所以我自己做的这版做了这些改动：

  • Wide enough to comfortably support the Duo both open and closed
    宽度足够，打开、合上都能稳稳撑住
  • MagSafe support with a lip so the Duo doesn’t rotate awkwardly from its width
    支持 MagSafe，并加了挡边，避免因机身宽度而别扭地旋转
  • The lip has a small groove specifically for the Duo that balances preventing the Duo or the stand from tipping over in portrait, while still making it easy to open and close
    挡边上有专为 Duo 做的浅槽，竖放时既不容易翻倒，又方便开合
  • The back of the stand extends up pretty much as far as it can go (without clipping the camera plateau when the device is closed) so that it has a lot of back support in portrait
    支架靠背尽量加高（合上时又不会碰到摄像头平台），竖放时背部支撑更足
  • Ports as well as speakers/mic are always open
    接口、扬声器和麦克风始终敞开
  • A really cool topographical pattern cut into the design, from areas around my home province of Nova Scotia
    支架上雕刻了很酷的地形纹理，取材自我家乡新斯科舍省附近的地貌

I also wanted to make it adaptable depending on your preferences, so I had Claude convert my Fusion document to OpenSCAD which has really rich variable support (much better than Fusion’s), so you can do stuff like:

我还希望它能按个人偏好调整，于是让 Claude 把我的 Fusion 文件转成了 OpenSCAD——它的变量支持丰富得多（比 Fusion 好用），这样你可以：

  • Disable the topographical pattern entirely (speeds up the print drastically as you don’t need to print lots of line detail to get the topographical pattern looking nice, but you miss out on the nice topographical pattern!)
    完全关掉地形纹理（打印会快很多，因为不用打那么多细线来表现纹理，当然也就没有那层漂亮的地形了）
  • Turns out my old MagSafe puck and my new one are slightly different diameters and Apple changed them in September 2024 when they bumped the speed up to 25W, so you can select which version you have for optimal fitment
    我发现旧款 MagSafe 充电盘和新款直径略有不同——Apple 在 2024 年 9 月把功率提升到 25W 时改过尺寸——你可以按自己手上的版本选择，以获得最佳贴合
  • You can even add an extra (or two) MagSafe pucks to the bottom surface, or swap one (or both) for an Apple Watch charger
    甚至可以在底面再加一个（或两个）MagSafe 充电盘，或者把其中一个（或两个）换成 Apple Watch 充电器

床头 StandBy 支架

While the previous stand can totally be used on your nightstand, I wanted something that let the phone sit at a steeper angle so it was easier to see in bed, and I never use Duo in “open mode” on the nightstand so I could also make it a fair bit smaller.

上一款支架完全可以用在床头，但我想要一个角度更陡、躺在床上看更轻松的；而且我从不会在床头把 Duo 打开用，所以这款也可以做得小得多。

My existing stand I used for my old iPhone Air and 15 Pro does not work with the Duo since it’s a little wide and the edge of the stand smacks into the Duo’s camera plateau when it’s in the horizontal, closed StandBy Mode orientation.

我以前给 iPhone Air 和 15 Pro 用的床头支架不适合 Duo：它稍微宽了一点，横放合上进入 StandBy 时，支架边缘会撞上 Duo 的摄像头平台。

So my goals for this stand were:

所以这款支架的目标是：

  • Barely larger than the MagSafe puck itself, so clearance with the camera plateau is a non issue
    尺寸几乎只比 MagSafe 充电盘大一点，这样就不必担心和摄像头平台打架
  • Have an L stand looking like the old iMacs/Cinema Displays so when the Duo is attached in landscape the Duo looks like it’s floating. This took quite a few tries to ensure the cable coming out the top both didn’t collide with the camera plateau and had enough of a radius to bend nicely
    做成老款 iMac / Cinema Display 那样的 L 形支架，横放吸附时 Duo 看起来像悬在空中。线缆从顶部伸出既不能碰到摄像头平台，又要有足够的弯曲半径，这花了好几轮尝试
  • Have the cable tuck in tidily, so there’s a little clip to keep the cable in place
    线缆要收纳整齐，所以加了一个小卡扣把线固定住

I’d recommend using some double-sided tape to really lock the MagSafe puck in place, as even though it has a nice, tight friction fit, if it warms up while charging I had it pull the puck loose once or twice when picking the phone up.

建议再用双面胶把 MagSafe 充电盘固定死。虽然过盈配合已经挺紧，但充电发热后，我有一两次拿起手机时把充电盘一起带松了。
