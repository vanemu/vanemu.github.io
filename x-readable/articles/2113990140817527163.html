上线前让 Opus 派 5 个子代理把应用「搞坏」

作者：@RoundtableSpace（0xMarioNawfal）
原文：https://x.com/RoundtableSpace/status/2113990140817527163

Before real users touch your app, have Opus 5.5 send in 5 subagents to break it.

在真实用户碰你的应用前，让 Opus 5.5 派 5 个子代理去搞坏它。

Run your app locally, open Claude Code in the project, and paste:

本地跑应用，在项目里打开 Claude Code，粘贴：

"Act as my break-it lead. Start the app, then spin up 5 subagents in parallel, each trying to break it a different way:

「当我的破环队长。启动应用，再并行起 5 个子代理，各自用不同方式搞坏它：

・ The chaos user: double clicks, the back button mid-checkout, refreshing during a save, 10 tabs open at once

混乱用户：双击、结账中途按返回、保存时刷新、一次开 10 个标签

・ The weird input user: emoji names, 5,000-character fields, pasted spreadsheets, wrong formats, empty submits

奇葩输入用户：emoji 名字、5000 字符字段、粘贴表格、错误格式、空提交

・ The slow user: a throttled network, timeouts, a flaky connection, a phone-size screen

慢用户：限速网络、超时、不稳连接、手机尺寸屏

・ The sneaky user: other people's IDs in the URL, edited requests, skipped steps, expired links

狡猾用户：URL 里塞别人的 ID、改请求、跳步骤、过期链接

・ The first-timer: no instructions, wrong turns, gives up the moment something is confusing

新手：无说明、走错路、一困惑就放弃

Each one logs every bug with exact steps to reproduce, what they expected, what happened, and a severity.

每个都记录每个 bug：精确复现步骤、期望、实际、严重级别。

Then merge everything into one list sorted by severity, fix the critical bugs, and re-run those tests to prove they're fixed."

再合并成按严重度排序的清单，修关键 bug，重跑那些测试证明已修好。」

Your users will find these bugs anyway. Find them first.

用户反正会发现这些 bug。先你自己发现。
