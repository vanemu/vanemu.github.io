Paywall

付费墙

The key is making the user feel like the app is being built specifically for THEM before they ever see the paywall. Use there name throughout onboarding and epically on the paywall title

关键是：在用户看到付费墙之前，让他们觉得这个应用是专门为「他们」打造的。在整个 onboarding 里反复用他们的名字，尤其是付费墙标题。
