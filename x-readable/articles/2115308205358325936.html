Left 评价流：更新后 What's New 再引导评分

作者：@CristianRus4 (cristian rus)
原文：https://x.com/CristianRus4/status/2115308205358325936

Left has over 2.8K reviews on the App Store, with a 4.7 rating

Left 在 App Store 已有超过 2800 条评价，评分 4.7。

my method for getting reviews is simple and it works. no dodgy tricks. it respects Apple's guidelines and the user experience, and you don't need to measure and stress with "aha! moments" or reward triggers

我拿评价的方法很简单，而且管用。没有歪门邪道，尊重 Apple 指南和用户体验，也不用纠结 aha moment 或奖励触发去量、去焦虑。

I tried all of that before and honestly I don't think any of it worked. the biggest jump in reviews (and in positive ones) came in the last few months, after this change, I know because I track it

这些我以前都试过，老实说觉得都不怎么管用。评价数量（以及好评）最大的跃升就发生在最近几个月、改了这个流程之后——我有在追踪，所以知道。

here's how:

做法如下：

・ make a "What's New?" screen that shows after major updates. not after every update, only a few times a year, when you ship something big like a major new feature. I version my app as 2026.XX.YY so whenever XX changes it appears this screen

做一个 What's New 屏，在重大更新后展示。不是每次更新，一年只几次，当你上线大功能时。我把版本写成 2026.XX.YY，所以只要 XX 变了就会出现这屏。

・ make your own review screen. show reviews from other users and community proof (if you have them!)

做自己的评价引导屏。展示其他用户的评价和社区证明（如果你有的话）。

・ connect the two. show the review screen right after the "What's New?" screen every time, unless the user is in a cooldown because they already left a review

把两者接起来。每次 What's New 之后立刻出评价屏，除非用户因已评过而在冷却中。

this gets you 5 stars almost every time because:

几乎总能拿到 5 星，因为：

・ it never interrupts someone while they're actually using the app

从不在用户真正使用 App 时打断他们

・ it shows up right after good news about new features, which shows you care about the app

刚好出现在关于新功能的好消息之后，显得你在乎产品

・ only people who actually use your app see it, because no one else opens the app after an update

只有真正在用的人会看到——更新后没人用的人不会打开 App

two more tips:

再多两个提示：

・ notice the "Send feedback" button under the CTA button, pretty explanatory

注意 CTA 按钮下面的 Send feedback——用意很清楚

・ after rating, people can close the screen or tap the CTA button again. it now says "Write a review", so they can add a written review if they want. never force it

打完分后，用户可以关掉屏幕，或再点一次 CTA；这时文案变成 Write a review，想写文字评价再写。永远不要强迫。

try it and let me know how it goes!

试试看，告诉我效果如何！
