1. Understanding the App Store Hierarchy

1. 理解 App Store 的层级

First of all, you need to understand how the App Store works.

首先，你得搞懂 App Store 怎么运作。

Not every field on your App Store listing has the same importance when it comes to keywords.

上架页里，不是每个字段对关键词的重要性都一样。

Some fields are generally considered more influential than others.

有些字段通常被认为影响力更大。

And no, putting your keywords everywhere isn't going to magically make you rank #1.

而且，把关键词塞得到处都是，并不会魔法般让你排到第一。

Here's how I think about it:

我大致是这么看的：

The App Title

App 标题

This is one of the most important places to put your main keyword. Apple gives you 30 characters, so don't waste them.

这是放主关键词最重要的位置之一。Apple 只给 30 个字符，别浪费。

If your app is a habit tracker, you probably want people searching for "habit tracker" to understand what your app does immediately.

如果你做的是习惯打卡类 App，大概希望搜「habit tracker」的人一眼就明白你的 App 干什么。

The Subtitle

副标题

The subtitle also plays an important role.

副标题也很重要。

You get another 30 characters to explain what your app does and target relevant keywords.

你还有 30 个字符，用来说明 App 做什么，并覆盖相关关键词。

It's generally considered less influential than the title, but still very important.

一般认为它不如标题权重高，但依然非常关键。

The Keywords Field

关键词字段

Here you get 100 characters.

这里有 100 个字符。

And trust me, those 100 characters are worth using properly.

相信我，这 100 个字符值得好好用。

You want to include relevant keywords that people are actually searching for.

你要放进用户真正会搜的相关词。

Apple can also combine words from your metadata to match different searches, so you don't necessarily need to write every possible keyword combination.

Apple 也会把元数据里的词组合起来匹配不同搜索，所以不必写出每一种可能的词组。

I'll explain this better in a second.

稍后我会讲得更清楚。

Promotional Text & Description

宣传文本与描述

Here's something that might save you some time.

有一点可能帮你省时间。

On Apple's App Store, the description and promotional text aren't direct keyword-ranking fields.

在 Apple 的 App Store 上，描述和宣传文本并不是直接的关键词排名字段。

So stop stuffing them with keywords, lol.

所以别再往里面堆关键词了，哈哈。

That doesn't mean they're useless.

这不代表它们没用。

Your description should explain why someone should download your app, while promotional text can highlight updates or important features.

描述应该说清「为什么该下载」；宣传文本可以强调更新或重要功能。

Just remember: we're trying to convince real people to download our apps, not only algorithms.

记住：我们是在说服真人下载，而不只是算法。

And one more thing: this is about Apple's App Store. Google Play works differently, especially when it comes to descriptions.

还有：这说的是 Apple App Store。Google Play 不一样，尤其是描述字段。

我见过的一个常见错误：漂亮品牌名放最前面，却不说明 App 到底做什么。

是的，我懂。

但问题在这。

Duolingo has the luxury of putting its brand first because millions of people already know what Duolingo is.

Duolingo 有本钱把品牌放前面，因为几百万人已经知道它是什么。

我们还没到那一步。

比如：

MyApp: Habit Tracker

这不是什么关键词靠前就保证排名更好的魔法。

但它能立刻告诉用户 App 做什么——在搜索结果里滑动时，这很重要。

Keep the title natural, relevant, and within Apple's 30-character limit.

标题要自然、相关，并控制在 Apple 的 30 字符限制内。

3. Stop Wasting Your 100 Keyword Characters

3. 别再浪费那 100 个关键词字符

Now let's talk about the keywords field.

接下来谈关键词字段。

For each localization, Apple gives you 100 characters, and you should try to make good use of them.

每个本地化语言，Apple 给你 100 个字符，应该好好用。

But here's something interesting, you don't always have to enter complete search phrases.

有趣的是：你不一定总要填完整搜索短语。

For example, let's say you have a habit tracker.

比如你做习惯打卡。

Instead of writing:

与其写成：

"habit tracker, habit goals, habit routine"

「habit tracker, habit goals, habit routine」

You could use individual relevant words separated by commas, avoiding words you've already included in your title or subtitle.

不如用逗号分隔的单个相关词，并避开标题、副标题里已经用过的词。

Something like:

类似：

"routine,goals,daily,streaks,planner"

「routine,goals,daily,streaks,planner」

Why?

为什么？

Because Apple can combine keywords from different fields and match multiple search queries.

因为 Apple 会组合不同字段里的关键词，匹配多种搜索。

So you're potentially targeting more searches without wasting characters repeating the same words.

这样你能覆盖更多搜索，又不用浪费字符重复同样的词。

Of course, that doesn't mean every possible combination will rank.

当然，这不代表每种组合都会有排名。

You still need to choose relevant keywords, and some exact phrases may be worth keeping together.

你仍要选相关词；有些精确短语也值得整段保留。

But in general:

但总体原则是：

Don't waste characters repeating words you've already used.

别浪费字符去重复已经用过的词。

Oh, and one more thing.

对了，还有一点。

Don't just target the keywords with the highest search volume.

别只盯搜索量最高的词。

If you're competing against apps with millions of downloads, good luck ranking above them from day one.

如果你要跟下载量上百万的 App 硬刚，想从第一天就排在它们上面？祝你好运。

Sometimes a smaller keyword with less competition is a much better opportunity.

有时搜索量更小、竞争更弱的词，才是更好的机会。

More about that later.

这点后面再说。

——信任。——

想象你找到两款类似的 App。

另一款 3.2 分、几乎没评论。

我自己的 App 也在这方面努力。

你可以做的一件事：在 App 内直接请用户评价。

但请别在第一次打开后立刻问。

那很烦人。

等到他们真正体验到有用的东西再说。

做出大家愿意用的东西，并让他们容易分享体验。

6. Localization Doesn't Mean Translating Word by Word

6. 本地化 ≠ 逐词翻译

This is probably one of the most interesting things I've learned recently.

这大概是我最近学到最有意思的一点。

I've asked quite a few developers how they localize their apps.

我问过不少开发者怎么做本地化。

And most of them were like:

大多数人差不多会说：

"Well, I just translate everything from English."

「嗯，我就把英文全翻过去。」

That's it.

就这样。

But here's the problem.

但问题在这。

People in different countries don't necessarily search for the same things.

不同国家的人，搜的东西未必一样。

Even when they're looking for exactly the same type of app.

即便他们要找的是同一类 App。

Let's say your English keyword is:

假设你的英文关键词是：

"Budget Tracker"

「Budget Tracker」

You can't automatically assume that its literal translation is the most searched term in Germany, Italy, or France.

你不能自动假设它的字面翻译就是德国、意大利或法国搜索量最高的词。

Maybe people use another expression.

也许当地人用另一个说法。

Maybe they search for something more specific.

也许他们搜得更具体。

Or maybe they use an English keyword even though their native language is different.

或者母语不同，却仍在用英文关键词。

I've personally noticed this while researching keywords for my app, Smoke Off.

我给自己的 App Smoke Off 做关键词研究时就亲身遇到过。

Some English keywords were getting searched in Italy, and that opened up opportunities I hadn't considered before.

有些英文词在意大利也有搜索量，打开了我之前没想过的机会。

So here's what I'd suggest.

所以我的建议是。

Before localizing:

本地化之前：

Research the keywords people actually use in that market.

研究那个市场里人们实际在用的关键词。

Compare their popularity and difficulty.

比较热度与难度。

Choose the ones that make sense for your app.

选对你 App 有意义的词。

Adapt your title, subtitle, and keywords accordingly.

相应调整标题、副标题和关键词字段。

Don't forget to localize your screenshots too.

别忘了截图也要本地化。

You can use ASO tools like Minimalist Screens to research keywords across different markets.

可以用 Minimalist Screens 这类 ASO 工具，跨市场做关键词研究。

Or, if you don't have access to a tool yet, ask an AI to help you brainstorm relevant keywords in a particular language.

或者如果还没有工具，可以让 AI 帮你用某种语言头脑风暴相关词。

Just don't blindly trust the suggestions.

只是别盲目相信建议。

AI can give you ideas, but it doesn't automatically know which keywords have the best search volume or competition.

AI 能给点子，但不会自动知道哪些词搜索量最好、竞争如何。

You'll still need to validate them.

你仍需要验证。

Also, keep in mind that App Store localizations depend on supported languages and storefronts, not simply one independent keyword field for every country.

另外记住：App Store 本地化取决于支持的语言与店面，不是每个国家都有独立关键词字段。

So check which languages Apple supports in your target markets.

所以先查清目标市场 Apple 支持哪些语言。

The main point is simple:

核心很简单：

Translate for how people search, not just how words are written.

按人们怎么搜来翻译，而不只是按字面怎么写。

7. Localize Your Pricing Too

7. 定价也要本地化

Now here's something that some developers might disagree with me on.

接下来这一点，有些开发者可能不同意我。

I think you should seriously consider localizing your pricing.

我认为你应该认真考虑本地化定价。

Unless you have significant AI costs, infrastructure costs, or other expenses that make it difficult.

除非你有很高的 AI 成本、基础设施成本或其他开支，让这变得很难。

Think about it.

想想看。

Someone living in a country with lower purchasing power might love your app but find your subscription too expensive.

住在购买力较低国家的人可能很喜欢你的 App，但觉得订阅太贵。

And you might be thinking:

你或许会想：

"But I want to make exactly €10 from every subscription."

「但我希望每笔订阅都刚好赚到 €10。」

Yeah, I get it.

嗯，我懂。

But would you rather get €5 from someone who otherwise wouldn't subscribe, or €0?

但你更想从本来不会订阅的人那里拿到 €5，还是 €0？

I know what I'd choose.

我知道我会选哪个。

Less money is better than no money.

少赚总比不赚好。

Of course, you still need to make sure you're profitable.

当然，你仍要确保自己是赚钱的。

Here's a simple framework I'd start testing with:

这是我会先拿来测试的一个简单框架：

MarketStarting Price
Tier 1100% of your base price
Tier 255–70% of your base price
Tier 330–40% of your base price

These are just starting points, not universal rules.

这些只是起点，不是放之四海的规则。

You shouldn't randomly throw countries into tiers either.

也不该随便把国家丢进某个档位。

Research purchasing power, local competitors, subscription behavior, and your actual costs.

要研究购买力、本地竞品、订阅习惯，以及你的真实成本。

For example, if your base subscription is €10/month, you might test a lower price in a market where €10 is relatively expensive.

例如基础订阅是 €10/月，你可以在 €10 相对偏贵的市场测试更低价。

Apple already handles currency conversions and certain tax adjustments, but that's not the same as optimizing your prices for purchasing power.

Apple 已经处理货币换算和部分税费调整，但那不等于按购买力优化价格。

With Minimalist Screens, you can explore localized pricing and adjust your pricing strategy for different markets.

用 Minimalist Screens，你可以探索本地化定价，并为不同市场调整策略。

I think it's worth experimenting with, especially when you're trying to reach users outside your main market.

我觉得值得试，尤其当你想触达主市场之外的用户时。

8. How I Actually Do Keyword Research

8. 我实际怎么做关键词研究

Okay, we talked about keywords a lot.

好了，关键词谈了很多。

But how do you actually find the right ones?

但怎么真正找到对的词？

There are plenty of ASO tools out there, and you can use whichever works best for you.

ASO 工具很多，选你最顺手的就行。

Personally, I built Minimalist Screens with my brother because I wanted to have everything in one place, without paying crazy prices for multiple tools.

就我个人而言，我和哥哥一起做了 Minimalist Screens，因为我想把一切放在一个地方，而不必为多个工具付天价。

We wanted to make ASO more accessible and affordable for indie developers like us.

我们想让 ASO 对像我们这样的独立开发者更易得、更便宜。

Here's how I approach keyword research.

我做关键词研究的大致方式是这样。

First, I look for relevant keywords.

首先，找相关关键词。

I start with what my app actually does.

从我的 App 实际做什么出发。

For Smoke Off, for example, I'd research terms related to quitting smoking, tracking cigarettes, cravings, and similar topics.

以 Smoke Off 为例，我会研究戒烟、记录抽烟、烟瘾等相关词。

The idea is to find keywords with real search intent.

目标是找到有真实搜索意图的词。

Then I look at popularity.

然后看热度。

Obviously, we want keywords that people are searching for.

显然我们要人们正在搜的词。

There's no point ranking #1 for a keyword nobody searches.

排第一但没人搜，毫无意义。

After that, I check difficulty.

接着看难度。

This is where it gets interesting.

这里开始有意思了。

A keyword might have amazing popularity, but if the competition is too strong, it might be almost impossible for a new app to rank well.

某个词可能热度很高，但竞争太强时，新 App 几乎不可能排得好。

That's why I prefer finding a balance between demand and competition.

所以我更偏好在需求与竞争之间找平衡。

Finally, I look at opportunity.

最后看机会。

Minimalist Screens has an opportunity metric that helps me compare keywords based on their potential.

Minimalist Screens 有一个 opportunity 指标，帮我按潜力比较关键词。

It makes it easier to decide which keywords are worth targeting and which ones I should probably leave alone for now.

更容易决定哪些值得做、哪些暂时该放过。

My general strategy is pretty simple:

我的总体策略很简单：

Find relevant keywords with decent demand and competition that my app has a realistic chance of handling.

找相关、有一定需求、且竞争程度是我的 App 现实上能对付的词。

I don't always go for the biggest ones.

我不总是冲最大的那些。

Especially when the app is still small.

尤其 App 还小时。

You can always target more competitive keywords as your app grows.

App 长大后，再去碰更卷的词也不迟。

9. Be Patient, Track Your Keywords, and Keep Improving

9. 要有耐心，追踪关键词，持续改进

One last thing.

最后再说一件事。

ASO takes time.

ASO 需要时间。

Please don't change your title today and expect to wake up tomorrow with a million downloads.

请别今天改标题，就指望明天醒来多出一百万下载。

You need to test things, understand what's working, and keep improving.

你需要测试、理解什么有效，并持续改进。

That's why tracking your keywords is important.

所以追踪关键词很重要。

For example, let's say your app is ranking #47 for a keyword.

比如你的 App 某词排第 47。

After making some changes, you move to #28.

改了之后到第 28。

Then #16.

再到第 16。

That's useful information.

这是有用的信息。

It doesn't necessarily mean every improvement came from your metadata changes, but at least you can start understanding how your visibility is changing.

不一定每次提升都来自元数据改动，但至少能开始理解可见度怎么在变。

And if your rankings aren't improving?

如果排名没改善呢？

Maybe your keywords are too competitive.

也许关键词太卷。

Maybe you're targeting the wrong search intent.

也许瞄准的搜索意图不对。

Or maybe there are better opportunities you haven't found yet.

或者还有更好的机会你还没找到。

That's why I added keyword tracking to Minimalist Screens.

所以我在 Minimalist Screens 里加了关键词追踪。

You can monitor your rankings over time instead of manually searching for your app every single day.

你可以随时间监控排名，而不必每天手动搜自己的 App。

But don't forget to measure actual results too.

但别忘了也要衡量实际结果。

Better rankings are nice.

排名变好很不错。

More downloads are even nicer.

下载变多更棒。

And more paying users?

付费用户更多？

Well, that's the goal lol.

嗯，那才是目标哈哈。

10. Don't Forget About the Actual App

10. 别忘了 App 本身

Before wrapping this up, there's one more thing I want to say.

收尾前还有一件事想说。

You can have amazing screenshots.

你可以有超棒的截图。

Perfectly optimized keywords.

完美优化的关键词。

Great localization.

出色的本地化。

And even a really good pricing strategy.

甚至很好的定价策略。

But if your app is terrible and nobody actually wants it, ASO isn't going to save you.

但如果 App 很糟、没人真正想要，ASO 救不了你。

I know that's not what everyone wants to hear.

我知道这话不是人人爱听。

But it's true.

但它是真的。

ASO helps people discover your app and can improve your chances of converting them into users.

ASO 帮人发现你的 App，也能提高转化成用户的概率。

It doesn't replace having a product that solves a real problem.

它替代不了「解决真实问题」的产品。

So keep improving your app while working on your distribution.

所以一边做分发，一边继续打磨产品。

You need both.

两者都要。
