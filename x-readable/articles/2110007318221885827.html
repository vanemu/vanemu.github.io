用 Apple Ads 约 30 天做到月入 $2.5k

作者：@frederickjames（Frederick James）
原文：https://x.com/frederickjames/status/2110007318221885827

I got to $2.5k/m in ~30 days with Apple ads.

我用 Apple Ads 大约 30 天做到了月入 $2.5k。

・ You get $100 from apple for free.

苹果会送你 $100 免费额度。

・ It's still the easiest marketing meta.

这仍然是最轻松的获客打法之一。

This is what I'd do today:

如果是今天，我会这样干：

・ Use [strict] keywords only.

只使用 [strict] 精确关键词。

・ Keywords = competitor's name.

关键词 = 竞品名。

・ Split campaigns by territory group.

按地区分组拆分广告系列。

・ Adjust CPT for each (i.e US is more).

按地区调整 CPT（比如美国更贵）。

・ Use the recommended CPT from apple.

使用苹果推荐的 CPT。

・ Make sure you have a cracked app store page.

确保你的 App Store 页面很能打。

・ Hire someone to do your screenshots or use AI.

请人做截图，或用 AI。

・ Use the new store assets!! Search banners etc.

用上新的商店素材！！搜索横幅等。

・ Budget about $25/day at first, increase ongoing.

一开始预算大约 $25/天，再逐步加。

・ Wait to see how the ads spend over 2 days.

等两天看广告怎么花。

・ No spend = competition spending more.

没消耗 = 对手出价更高。

・ Increase the CPT until you find a good balance.

提高 CPT，直到找到合适平衡。

・ Export data, give to Claude to find winners.

导出数据，交给 Claude 找赢家。

・ Scale those further.

继续放大那些赢家。

Note:

注意：

Apple ads is like ASO, it won't work if your niche is insanely saturated. The ideal situation: one big app doing organic, where people search for the name (and keyword) and then get shown our hyper targeted ad.

Apple Ads 像 ASO：赛道极度饱和时不好使。理想情况是：有一个大应用靠自然搜索，用户搜它的名字（和关键词）时，会看到你的超精准广告。

Another point: some industries won't work for indie devs. For example, finance. Big companies will buy their own keywords at $25 per tap just to spite the competition. Positioning matters way more with Apple ads.

还有一点：有些行业不适合独立开发者。比如金融。大公司会以每次点击 $25 的价格买自己的关键词，纯粹为了挤死对手。定位在 Apple Ads 里比什么都重要。
