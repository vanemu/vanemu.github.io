1. Target the card in hand, not the curve

1. 瞄准手里有卡的人，不是曲线

Most SEO traffic is vanity.

多数 SEO 流量是虚荣。

"What is SEO". "CBD benefits". Your Search Console curve goes up, you feel great, and nobody buys. Paul puts it bluntly: is that traffic for your ego or for your business?

「What is SEO」「CBD benefits」。Search Console 曲线往上，你很爽，但没人买。Paul 说得很直：这流量是喂自尊，还是喂生意？

The only keywords worth your first six months are the ones where the searcher already has the card in hand. "SEO consultant Paris". "Coffee table". "Shopify SEO agency".

头六个月唯一值得做的词，是搜索者手里已经握着卡的那些：「SEO consultant Paris」「Coffee table」「Shopify SEO agency」。

You do not need a tool to read intent. Google already did the testing. Type the keyword and look at what ranks.

读意图不需要工具。Google 已经测过了。打那个词，看谁在排。

-> Blog posts and a definition box at the top: informational. Skip for now. -> Landing pages, service pages, category pages with prices: commercial. That is your target.

→ 顶部是博客和定义框：信息型。先跳过。 → 落地页、服务页、带价格的类目页：商业型。那才是目标。

Then think in patterns, not keywords. A keyword gets you a page. A pattern gets you a machine. Consultant + city. Agency + CMS. Product type + material. Each one replicates as far as the market goes.

然后按模式想，不要按单个词想。一个词给你一页；一个模式给你一台机器。顾问+城市。机构+CMS。产品类型+材质。市场有多远，模式就能复制多远。

The free way to find them is Google Suggest. Type your seed, then add a * at the end, then at the start. Each one surfaces a different batch. Google Ads' Keyword Planner adds the volumes.

免费挖法是 Google Suggest：打种子词，再在末尾或开头加 *。每一种都会冒出不同一批。Google Ads 的 Keyword Planner 再补量级。

The fast way is to let the Chief ask the analyst.

快法：让「总监」去问分析师。

Step 6 is there for a reason. Claude will happily fill a blank column with a plausible number. The analyst exists so it never has to.

第 6 步有原因：Claude 很乐意往空列填一个像样的数字。有分析师，它就不必瞎编。

2. One keyword, one page. Decide before you write.

2. 一词一页。动笔前先定。

The most expensive SEO mistake is writing a page you already had.

最贵的 SEO 错误，是写你其实已经有的那一页。

Here is the check Paul does by hand. Search Console, Performance, filter on the exact query, then the Pages tab.

Paul 手工检查是这样：Search Console → Performance → 精确查询过滤 → Pages 标签。

-> A page already gets clicks for it: optimise that page. -> A page gets plenty of impressions but few clicks: it is already somewhere in the top 10. Optimise it. -> Nothing: create a new page.

→ 已有页面在吃点击：优化那页。 → 展示很多、点击很少：它已经在前十某处。优化它。 → 什么都没有：新建一页。

Get this wrong and you build cannibalisation. Two of your pages fight for the same query and Google cannot choose. Paul's example: both sit 7th and 8th, when one page would have been 3rd.

搞错就会自相残杀：两页抢同一查询，Google 选不了。Paul 的例子：两页分别排 7、8，本来一页能到 3。

Then the part most consultants skip: secondary keywords. Take whoever ranks 1st for your keyword and list every other query that exact URL ranks top 5 for. That list is what Google already considers the same page. Target it all with one URL.

然后多数顾问跳过的部分：次要词。把该词排第 1 的 URL 拿出来，列出它还在前五的其他查询。那份列表就是 Google 已认定「同属一页」的东西——用同一个 URL 全打。

It all lands in the title. The title is the page's identity card, the strongest on-page element you have. One word decides whether a page ranks for one query or five. "SEO Consultant Paris (Freelance)" covers two patterns at once. If pixels remain, spend them on the click: a rating, a star, a reason to pick you over the nine other results.

这一切落在标题上。标题是页面身份证，你最强的 on-page 元素。一个词决定一页打一个查询还是五个。「SEO Consultant Paris (Freelance)」一次盖两种模式。像素还有剩，就花在点击上：评分、星星、选你而不是另外九个结果的理由。

The meta description barely moves rankings. Its job is to sell the click.

meta description 几乎不动排名。它的工作是卖点击。

When Paul ran this live on his Lyon consultant page, ChatSEO flagged a canonical tag he had never noticed. He has been doing SEO since 2021. That is what the analyst is for.

Paul 在里昂顾问页现场跑时，ChatSEO 标出他从没注意过的 canonical。他从 2021 年做 SEO——分析师就是干这个的。

3. Build the page the top 3 are almost building

3. 做「前三名几乎在做」的那一页，再更好一点

The best on-page advice is also the least technical: open the top 3 and put yourself in the searcher's head.

最好的 on-page 建议也最不技术：打开前三，把自己放进搜索者脑子。

Someone typing "SEO consultant Paris" wants to know why you, what it costs, how it works and how long until results. Answer those, in the order the SERP answers them, and you are level.

打「SEO consultant Paris」的人想知道：为什么是你、多少钱、怎么做、多久见效。按 SERP 回答它们的顺序答完，你就打平了。

Then do it better. Paul's teardown of a competitor ranking 1st: no photo of the person you would hire, and no way to self-qualify. Add the face. Add a three-question quiz, "Am I the right consultant for you". People stay, they interact, Google reads it.

然后再更好。Paul 拆过一个排第 1 的对手：没有你会雇的那个人的照片，也没有自助筛选。加上脸。加一个三问测验「我是不是适合找你」。人会停留、互动，Google 读得见。

If you want to see what "better" looks like, study SERPs where the money is brutal, even outside your niche. Debt consolidation, finance, CBD. Infographics, comparison tables, embedded video. That is the ceiling.

想看「更好」长什么样，去看钱很凶的 SERP，哪怕跨行业：债务整合、金融、CBD。信息图、对比表、内嵌视频——那是天花板。

The rest of on-page is mostly noise.

其余 on-page 大多是噪音。

One H1 with your primary keyword. The H2 and H3 tree takes care of itself when Claude writes the page, so do not spend a weekend on it. Structured data, one instruction and forget it.

一个带主词的 H1。H2/H3 树 Claude 写页时会自己长出来，别花周末抠。结构化数据：一条指令然后忘掉。

Internal links are a threshold, not a lever. Google discovers pages by clicking every link from your homepage like an idiot. A page nothing links to barely exists. Paul's floor is five internal links to every new page, from pages on a close topic. Your money pages go in the header. The long tail goes in the footer. The best ones sit inside body text, because the words around the link add context. Internal anchors can be the exact keyword. That freedom stops at your own domain.

内链是门槛，不是杠杆。Google 像傻子一样从首页点每一个链接来发现页面。没人链到的页几乎不存在。Paul 的底线：每张新页至少五条内链，来自相近主题页。赚钱页进页头；长尾进页脚；最好的放正文里，因为锚点周围的词给上下文。站内锚文本可以是精确关键词——这份自由停在你自己的域名边界。

The day Claude deleted the rankings

Claude 删掉排名的那天

Paul plugged Claude into his site to edit his city pages.

Paul 把 Claude 接到站点上改城市页。

Recording a podcast, he went to show the "best SEO consultants" ranking on one of those pages. It was gone. He opened the Paris page. Gone too.

录播客时，他想展示某页上的「最佳 SEO 顾问」排名——没了。打开巴黎页——也没了。

Claude had rewritten the pages and dropped the ranking tables. No error, no warning. And those rankings are the bait for the GEO swaps in section 4.

Claude 重写了页面，把排名表丢掉了。没报错，没警告。而那些排名正是第 4 节 GEO 互换的诱饵。

An agent that can edit your live site can also quietly remove the one thing the page existed for. So our ship skill has a gate.

能改线上站的 Agent，也能悄悄拆掉页面存在的理由。所以我们的 ship skill 有一道闸。

Step 5 is the whole lesson. The day it catches nothing is the day you stop needing it, and that day does not come.

第 5 步就是整堂课。有一天它抓不到任何问题，你才可以停用它——而那天还没来。

4. Authority, and why GEO is mostly SEO

4. 权威，以及为什么 GEO 多半还是 SEO

A perfect page on a weak domain ranks 40th. Nothing in section 3 fixes that.

弱域名上的完美页面排第 40。第 3 节什么都修不好这个。

Google does not give a new site the time of day for roughly six months. It has no reason to index every site Claude can generate in an afternoon. Authority is how you get past that. Links from other sites, and your brand showing up across the web: YouTube, LinkedIn, X, podcasts.

新站大约六个月 Google 不怎么给面子。它没理由索引 Claude 一下午能生成的每一个站。权威是你过关的方式：外链，以及品牌在网上现身——YouTube、LinkedIn、X、播客。

Every score for it is an estimate. Ahrefs has DR, Semrush has Authority Score, Majestic has Trust Flow. None of them is Google. Paul trusts Ahrefs DR the most, and it is the one ChatSEO shows you, but someone once told Paul, worried, that their DR dropped from 70 to 65. His answer: look at your Search Console. More clicks? On business keywords? More top 3? That is the scoreboard.

所有权威分数都是估计。Ahrefs 有 DR，Semrush 有 Authority Score，Majestic 有 Trust Flow。没有一个是 Google。Paul 最信 Ahrefs DR，ChatSEO 也显示它；但有人曾紧张地告诉 Paul DR 从 70 掉到 65。他的回答：看 Search Console。点击更多了吗？是生意词吗？前三更多了吗？那才是记分板。

Before you buy links, be patient. Google deliberately delays the effect of changes, so you cannot reverse-engineer it in a week. After a change, a dip is normal. Give it up to two months. Reverting in a panic is how you teach Google you are testing it.

买链之前先耐心。Google 故意延迟变化效果，免得你一周就反推规则。改完后下滑正常。给最多两个月。恐慌回滚等于教 Google 你在测它。

Still stuck after three months on a page you know is good? Then links.

一张你明知很好的页，三个月还卡住？再谈外链。

Buying them is its own profession. Marketplaces sell €300 links on sites that rank for nothing. What to look for: a site on your topic, and a linking page that itself ranks for something. Google already trusts that page, so the vote counts.

买链本身是一门专业。市场上有人卖 €300、却排不到任何词的站上的链接。该看什么：同主题的站，以及那条链出所在页本身也排着东西。Google 已信任那页，票才算数。

Anchors are where people get penalised. Google's spam policies list buying or selling links for ranking purposes as link spam. Theory says don't. Practice says if you do, leave no footprint, and a wall of exact-match anchors is the easiest footprint there is. From outside, mostly "here", your brand, your URL, "source". Exact-match only from your very best links.

锚文本是最容易被罚的地方。Google 垃圾政策把「为排名买卖链接」列为链接垃圾。理论说别做；实践说如果你做，别留脚印——整墙精确匹配锚文本是最容易的脚印。对外大多用「here」、品牌、URL、「source」。精确匹配只留给最好的几条链。

GEO is a Google query in disguise

GEO：披着皮的 Google 查询

GEO is a lot of hype and very little data.

GEO 炒作很多，数据很少。

In SEO, Google tells you what people search and how often. In GEO you cannot know the prompt. A plumber in Lille sends ChatGPT a voice note about ads being too expensive and wanting "a guy in my city I can meet".

SEO 里 Google 告诉你搜什么、多频繁；GEO 里你不知道 prompt。里尔一个水管工给 ChatGPT 发语音：广告太贵，想找「本市能见面的人」。

ChatGPT does not know the answer. So it searches Google. And it does not search the voice note. It searches something like "best SEO consultant Lille 2026". Best, because the user wants a recommendation. The year, because it wants fresh sources.

ChatGPT 不知道答案，于是搜 Google。它搜的不是语音原文，而类似「best SEO consultant Lille 2026」。best，因为要推荐；年份，因为要新鲜来源。

So GEO starts as SEO on "best + money keyword + year".

所以 GEO 起步就是「best + 赚钱词 + 年份」上的 SEO。

Add "best" and the year to the titles of your commercial pages. Publish rankings on those queries. Then the lever that really moves it: get other sites' rankings to list you.

商业页标题加上「best」和年份。围绕这些查询发排名内容。真正撬动的杠杆：让别人的排名列表写上你。

Ranking yourself 1st on your own site used to work. It does not really any more. What works is what Paul does with his rankings of agencies: agencies email to be included, he asks for no money, only that they list him, or ChatSEO, in a ranking on their own site. A third party saying it is the source ChatGPT cites.

自己站上把自己排第 1 曾经管用，现在不太行了。管用的是 Paul 做机构排名的做法：机构写信求收录，他不收钱，只要求对方也在自己站的排名里列他或 ChatSEO。第三方说出来，才是 ChatGPT 引用的来源。

5. Scale the pattern, one page a day

5. 放大模式，一天一页

This is where Claude Code stops being an assistant and becomes a department.

这时 Claude Code 不再是助手，而成了部门。

Once one page in a pattern is right, the next forty are the same page. Consultant + Lyon has the same intent as consultant + Lille. Paul's advice: tell Claude to do every major city. Then do not publish forty the same afternoon. On a new site that pattern screams automation.

模式里一页做对了，后面四十页就是同一页。顾问+里昂 与顾问+里尔 意图相同。Paul 的建议：让 Claude 把主要城市都做了。但别同一下午发四十页——新站上那模式像在喊「自动化」。

One page a day, on a schedule.

一天一页，按日程。

Put it on a routine (/schedule in Claude Code, or a plain cron) and it runs every morning without you.

挂到例行（Claude Code 的 /schedule，或普通 cron），每天早上不用你动手。

Then the part that compounds: the weekly review.

然后是会复利的部分：周复盘。

That is the loop. Paul's own site now takes him about ten minutes a week. The first six months will feel like nothing is happening. Then you hit a pivot where every new page ranks top 5 on arrival, and that is when you turn the drip up.

循环就是这样。Paul 自己的站现在每周大约十分钟。头六个月会感觉什么都没发生。然后你会撞到拐点：每张新页一上线就进前五——那时再把滴灌开大。

What this does not prove

这证明不了什么

None of this is magic, and I would rather say it than have it said in the replies.

这些都不是魔法。我宁可自己说，也不想在回复里被说。

Paul learned SEO in an agency and has been doing it since 2021. His site has years of authority and an audience that searches his name. We started with that advantage and you might not.

Paul 在机构学 SEO，从 2021 年做到现在。他的站有多年权威，还有人搜他名字。我们带着这个优势起步，你未必有。

On a fresh domain going after competitive head terms, this exact setup produces pages that rank 40th. The skills will not fix authority. They only make sure you do not waste it.

全新域名去抢竞争大词，同一套配置会产出排第 40 的页。Skills 补不了权威，只能确保你不浪费已有的权威。

And an agent with write access to your site is a risk. The diff gate exists because it already went wrong once. Keep it, even when it feels slow.

对站点有写权限的 Agent 是风险。diff 闸存在，是因为已经出事过一次。就算觉得慢，也留着。

The part I am confident generalises is the ordering. The part I am not confident generalises is the speed.

我确信可迁移的是顺序；不确定可迁移的是速度。

What I would do tomorrow on a new site

明天在新站我会怎么做

Install the ChatSEO MCP and connect Search Console before writing a single page Run money-keywords and delete every informational keyword it tempts you with Build one reference page per pattern by hand, with page-brief and ship-page Put pattern-drip on a daily routine, one page a day Run weekly-seo every Monday and never touch a page edited in the last 60 days Start geo-rankings in month three, once the pages worth citing exist

装 ChatSEO MCP，连 Search Console，再写第一页 / 跑赚钱词，删掉所有诱惑你的信息型词 / 每个模式亲手做一张参考页（page-brief + ship-page）/ 把 pattern-drip 挂成每日例行，一天一页 / 每周一跑 weekly-seo，且绝不碰最近 60 天改过的页 / 第三个月再开 geo-rankings，等值得被引用的页先存在

In that order, because each one only works if the previous one exists.

按这个顺序——因为每一步只有上一步存在才管用。

Pick the money. Decide before you write. Build better than the top 3. Wait. Repeat daily.

挑赚钱的。动笔前先定。做得比前三更好。等。每天重复。
