这是一份 10 步教程：从理解刚刚发生了什么，到跑起一队由 Opus 驱动的专家 bot，彼此交接工作——只在需要人类的时候把你拉进来。

01. Understand what just changed

01. 理解刚刚发生了什么

It started with one post. Late on October 6 US time, Musk wrote on X that SpaceX will use the best back-end model for any given task - naming Claude Opus 5.5, Midjourney and Suno. His closing line: "Whatever is most likely to give you the best outcome."

一切始于一条帖子。美国时间 10 月 6 日晚些时候，Musk 在 X 上写道：SpaceX 会为任何给定任务使用最好的后端模型——点名 Claude Opus 5.5、Midjourney 和 Suno。他的收尾句是：「无论什么最可能给你最好的结果。」

Hours later, Lauren (@poteto) from the Grok Bot team made it concrete: all bots will be powered by Opus 5.5, and they can spawn cloud agents running Cursor's models. 9to5Mac reported it live as of October 7.

几小时后，Grok Bot 团队的 Lauren（@poteto）说得更具体：所有 bot 都将由 Opus 5.5 驱动，并且它们可以拉起运行 Cursor 模型的云端 agent。9to5Mac 在 10 月 7 日作了现场报道。

02. Install it and meet the Chief

02. 安装它，认识 Chief

Grok Bot is an app, not a browser tab. It runs on Mac, iPhone and iPad. You sign in with your Grok or Cursor account - it is not sold on its own, it comes bundled with most SuperGrok and paid Cursor plans.

Grok Bot 是一个 App，不是浏览器标签页。它跑在 Mac、iPhone 和 iPad 上。你用 Grok 或 Cursor 账号登录——它不单独售卖，而是随大多数 SuperGrok 和付费 Cursor 套餐附带。

You land in something closer to a messaging client than a chatbot: named bots on the left, a conversation on the right. Each bot gets its own computer in the cloud.

你进入的界面更像即时通讯，而不是聊天机器人：左边是命名好的 bot，右边是对话。每个 bot 在云端有自己的电脑。

You do not need to switch anything to get Opus 5.5. According to the Grok Bot team, it is the default for every bot. There is no separate Claude subscription.

你不需要切换任何东西就能用上 Opus 5.5。据 Grok Bot 团队所说，它是每个 bot 的默认模型。不需要另开 Claude 订阅。

Start with one general-purpose bot - call it the Chief. Its job is to be the one you message when you do not yet know which specialist should own a task.

先从一个通用 bot 开始——叫它 Chief。它的工作是：当你还不知道该交给哪个专家时，你就先找它。

Give it a real errand with a result you can check in thirty seconds. Every step after this is about trusting it with bigger jobs - so start with one you can verify.

给它一件能在三十秒内核对结果的真实差事。之后的每一步，都是在把更大的工作托付给它——所以从你能验证的一件事开始。

03. Write a charter for a stronger brain

03. 给更强的大脑写一份章程（charter）

A prompt is a request. A bot is a role - it persists, builds memory, and owns a domain. So name it after a job: Inbox Manager, Research Lead, Sales Outbound.

提示词是一次请求。Bot 是一个角色——它会持续存在、积累记忆、并负责一个领域。所以用岗位来命名：收件箱经理、研究负责人、销售外联。

Then brief it like a new hire: what it owns, what good looks like, and where it must stop and ask you.

然后像带新人一样给它简报：它负责什么、什么叫做好、以及哪里必须停下来问你。

Here is what changes with Opus 5.5. Charters written for the old default were defensive - short chains, frequent check-ins - because the model stalled a few steps in. With a brain that scores almost 3x higher on multi-step work, you can hand over the whole job, not just the first step.

Opus 5.5 改变的是这些。为旧默认模型写的章程偏防守——链路短、频繁确认——因为模型走几步就会卡住。现在这个大脑在多步骤工作上几乎高出 3 倍，你可以交出整份工作，而不只是第一步。

Opus 5.5 also supports a 1M-token context window and up to 128K output tokens on the API. How much of that Grok Bot exposes is not published - but longer jobs are now the point.

Opus 5.5 在 API 上还支持 100 万 token 上下文窗口，以及最高 128K 输出 token。Grok Bot 暴露了其中多少尚未公开——但更长的任务现在才是重点。

The rule: a smarter brain gets more steps, not more authority. The "where you stop" block stays exactly as strict as before.

规则是：更聪明的大脑换来更多步骤，而不是更多权限。「你在哪里停下」那一块，严格程度要和以前完全一样。

04. Connect tools once - and draw the data line

04. 工具只连一次——并划清数据线

Grok Bot ships a plugin panel with one-click connections: Notion, Slack, Google Drive, AWS Agents, AWS SageMaker, Browserbase, Composio and Context7, plus custom ones.

Grok Bot 自带插件面板，一键连接：Notion、Slack、Google Drive、AWS Agents、AWS SageMaker、Browserbase、Composio 和 Context7，以及自定义插件。

Connections are account-level. Connect Gmail once, and every bot you create can use it. Your fifth bot is productive in seconds.

连接是账号级别的。Gmail 连一次，你创建的每个 bot 都能用。你的第五个 bot 几秒钟就能干活。

The new part: your data now has a second destination. When a bot thinks with Opus 5.5, what it reads goes to Anthropic for that step. Tom's opt-out question under Musk's post has no answer yet, and SpaceXAI has not published terms for what is shared.

新的一点：你的数据现在有了第二个去向。当 bot 用 Opus 5.5 思考时，它读到的内容会为那一步送到 Anthropic。Tom 在 Musk 帖子下关于 opt-out 的问题还没有答案，SpaceXAI 也尚未公布共享内容的条款。

You cannot choose where the model runs. You can choose what the bot touches. Add this to every charter:

你无法选择模型跑在哪里。你可以选择 bot 能碰什么。把这一点写进每一份章程：

Connect what you actually need, leave the rest alone. During a beta, every connection reaches every bot - and now more than one company.

只连接你真正需要的，其余别动。在 beta 期间，每个连接对每个 bot 都可用——而现在牵涉的不止一家公司。

05. Hand off the login, never the password

05. 交接登录界面，绝不交出密码

Most software inside a real company has no API and no MCP server. This is the mechanic that makes Grok Bot work on it anyway.

真实公司里的多数软件既没有 API，也没有 MCP 服务器。正是这套机制让 Grok Bot 依然能用上它们。

The bot navigates its own cloud browser until it hits a login wall. Then it hands you the screen. You sign in, click done, and the bot picks up on the same browser session where it left off.

Bot 在自己的云浏览器里导航，直到撞上登录墙。然后它把屏幕交给你。你登录、点完成，bot 在同一浏览器会话里从离开的地方继续。

The bot gets a session, not a secret. You never type a credential into chat - and with a third-party model now in the loop, that matters more than ever. A password pasted into a conversation is text the model reads. A login done on the handed-off screen is not.

Bot 拿到的是会话，不是秘密。你从不在聊天里输入凭证——现在链路里还有第三方模型，这一点比以往更重要。粘贴进对话的密码是模型会读到的文本。在交接屏幕上完成的登录则不是。

The rule to insist on: if any tool ever asks you to paste a password into a message, that is the wrong path.

必须坚持的规则：如果任何工具让你把密码粘贴进消息，那就是错误路径。

06. Show it once, then make it a routine

06. 演示一次，然后做成例程

You can teach a bot a workflow by doing it once while it watches. It saves the steps and runs them on its own next time.

你可以一边做、一边让 bot 看着，教它一个工作流。它会保存步骤，下次自己跑。

The best first recording is recurring, multi-tool and stable: something you do weekly, across two or more apps, where the steps rarely change. Tedious to describe, forty seconds to show.

最好的第一次录制，是周期性、跨多工具且稳定的：你每周都会做、跨两个或更多 App、步骤很少变的事。用语言描述很烦，演示只要四十秒。

Then give the routine a reason to fire. Two options, both set in plain language - no node canvas, no workflow builder:

然后给例程一个触发理由。两种选项都用自然语言设置——没有节点画布，没有工作流搭建器：

Where Opus 5.5 earns its keep: routines break when a site changes its layout or an input looks slightly different. A model that is stronger at multi-step work is more likely to recover from a changed page instead of silently failing.

Opus 5.5 真正赚回身价的地方：网站改了布局，或输入略有不同时，例程会坏。更擅长多步骤工作的模型，更可能从改版页面中恢复，而不是默默失败。

07. Hire specialists that spawn Cursor agents

07. 雇专家 bot，让它们拉起 Cursor agent

Run several bots in parallel, each owning one domain. Separate bots mean separate memory, separate context and a clean thread to check when something goes wrong. SpaceXAI says its own teams run bots for sales outreach, marketing, office operations and bug fixes.

并行跑多个 bot，每个负责一个领域。分开的 bot 意味着分开的记忆、分开的上下文，以及出问题时可检查的干净线程。SpaceXAI 说自己的团队用 bot 做销售外联、营销、办公室运营和修 bug。

Split by domain, not by task size. An Expense Manager that only thinks about receipts gets good at your receipts.

按领域拆分，而不是按任务大小。一个只想收据的费用经理，会变得很擅长处理你的收据。

The new layer is the quieter half of the Grok Bot team's announcement: bots can spawn cloud agents running any of Cursor's models. That fits the ownership map - SpaceX bought Cursor in August in a $60 billion deal.

新的一层，是 Grok Bot 团队公告里更安静的那一半：bot 可以拉起运行任意 Cursor 模型的云端 agent。这符合所有权版图——SpaceX 在八月以 600 亿美元交易收购了 Cursor。

So a coding bot becomes a manager. The Opus-powered bot plans the job and holds the context. Cursor agents do the heavy lifting in parallel. The bot reviews what comes back.

于是，一个写代码的 bot 变成了管理者。由 Opus 驱动的 bot 规划任务并握住上下文。Cursor agent 并行做重活。Bot 再审查回来的结果。

Users on X were already doing a version of this before the announcement - posted in late September about spinning up Cursor cloud agents from Grok Bot

公告之前，X 上已有用户在做类似的事——九月末发帖说从 Grok Bot 拉起 Cursor 云端 agent。

08. Put them in a group chat

08. 把它们放进群聊

Bots can message each other and share context in threads. Put several in one group chat and they coordinate on their own - passing work, assigning ownership, and pulling you in only for judgment calls.

Bot 可以互相发消息，并在线程里共享上下文。把几个放进同一个群聊，它们会自己协调——传递工作、分配所有权，只在需要判断时把你拉进来。

SpaceXAI's own example: an engineering bot reproduces a bug, files a ticket, and hands it to a second bot to debug. One bot deciding another is better suited - and passing ownership - is the thing no single-chat tool does.

SpaceXAI 自己的例子：工程 bot 复现 bug、建工单，再交给第二个 bot 去调试。一个 bot 判断另一个更合适——并移交所有权——这是单聊工具做不到的事。

Since late September, teams can also publish a shared bot that colleagues use in the app or in Slack.

九月末以来，团队也可以发布共享 bot，同事在 App 或 Slack 里使用。

The trick is to give the group an objective, not a task list. A task list means you already did the decomposition. An objective lets them split the work - and decomposition is exactly the kind of multi-step reasoning Opus 5.5 is strongest at.

窍门是给群组一个目标，而不是任务清单。任务清单意味着你已经做完了拆解。目标则让它们自己分工——而拆解正是 Opus 5.5 最擅长的那类多步骤推理。

09. Draw the approval line

09. 划清审批线

Grok Bot's premise is that a bot finishes jobs end to end and comes back only when something needs your approval. That puts the burden on you to define "needs approval" - because the bot's default may not match yours.

Grok Bot 的前提是：bot 端到端做完工作，只在需要你批准时回来。这把定义「需要批准」的负担放在了你身上——因为 bot 的默认未必符合你的标准。

The line that works is not about task size. It is about reversibility. Anything the bot can undo, it finishes alone. Anything the outside world sees, that moves money, or that cannot be taken back, gets parked for you.

管用的那条线，不关乎任务大小，而关乎可逆性。bot 能撤销的事，它独自做完。外界能看见的、会动钱的、或无法收回的事，先停下来等你。

This matters more now, not less. A stronger brain will push further into a job before it hits your line - so the line has to be explicit.

这一点现在更重要，而不是更不重要。更强的大脑会在撞到你的线之前走得更远——所以这条线必须明确。

The shape to aim for on every bot: 36 drafts queued, 0 sent. Every reversible step done, a hard stop at the first irreversible one.

每个 bot 都该瞄准的形态：36 份草稿排队，0 封已发送。所有可逆步骤做完，在第一个不可逆步骤硬停。

And not everything announced is live. Midjourney and Suno have no date, and at least one user's bot said Suno was not available on day one. Build on Opus 5.5 - it is confirmed. Wait on the rest.

而且并非所有宣布的能力都已上线。Midjourney 和 Suno 没有日期，至少有一个用户的 bot 在第一天说 Suno 不可用。先建在 Opus 5.5 上——它已确认。其余的等。

Then put fifteen minutes a week on the calendar and let the bots report on themselves:

然后每周在日历里留十五分钟，让 bot 自己汇报：

Spot-check one output per routine by hand. A bot grading its own work has the same blind spot you do.

每个例程亲手抽查一个产出。Bot 给自己的工作打分，盲点和你一样。

Conclusion: the harness is the product

结论：套件才是产品

For three years the question was which model is best. This week the most aggressive model-builder in the market answered a different one: which harness is best - and then put a rival's brain inside it.

三年来，问题一直是哪个模型最好。这一周，市场上最激进的模型制造商回答了另一个问题：哪个 harness 最好——然后把对手的大脑装了进去。

That is the shift. The harness owns the computer, the logins, the memory and the routines. The brain is swappable underneath, and right now the best one for agent work is Opus 5.5.

这就是转变。Harness 拥有电脑、登录态、记忆和例程。大脑可以在下面替换，而眼下最适合 agent 工作的，就是 Opus 5.5。

Your job does not change. You decide what to delegate, where the bot's authority ends, and what data it may touch. Write all three into the charter - and let the strongest brain on the market do the clicking.

你的工作没有变。你决定委派什么、bot 的权限止于何处、以及它可以碰哪些数据。把这三点都写进章程——然后让市场上最强的大脑去做点击。
